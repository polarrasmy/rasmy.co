The Project

Fountain of Youth is a Guy Ritchie-directed action-adventure for Apple TV+, starring John Krasinski and Charlize Theron. Pola Rasmy joined the camera and electrical department for principal photography in March 2024 — an official IMDb credit that placed Egyptian technical craft inside one of the year's biggest streaming productions.

What It Meant

It wasn't just a job. It was proof that the pipeline from Cairo to Hollywood works — that a director-DOP from Egypt could operate at the level of an international blockbuster crew and come back with the credit to show for it.

Official credit: Pola Raafat Rasmy Gindy — Camera and Electrical Department, Fountain of Youth (2024), Apple TV+.
