Pola Rasmy

Film Director. Director of Photography. Travel Filmmaker.

Based in Cairo, working between Egypt, China, and Hong Kong. Founder of RASMY Productions — a production company bridging Middle Eastern and Asian markets.

The Work

Started in 2018 building income through film services. Shot in Hollywood in March 2024 as part of Guy Ritchie's crew on Fountain of Youth for Apple TV+ — an official credit that put Egyptian craftsmanship on a global stage.

In June 2025, traveled to China for work that defined a new chapter: not just shooting for clients, but building systems, products, and infrastructure that scale.

The Shift

Today the focus is on what comes after the camera stops rolling. AI filmmaking pipelines. Local inference on mini computers. Agent orchestration for production workflows. Private AI infrastructure for companies that can't afford cloud exposure.

The goal: Egyptian-made AI products that compete globally — hardware and software built together, from proof of concept to product.
