About

Director. DOP. Builder between Cairo and China.

Pola Rasmy directs and shoots films that move brands — from automotive launches in the Gulf to destination work across Asia, with crews that span three continents.

It started with a camera in Cairo and a stubborn belief that a story shot on the right light beats a story shot on the biggest budget. Pola trained in mass communication and PR, then spent years on set — learning grip, lighting, and the quiet discipline of directing before ever calling himself one.

In March 2024 he shot on the Guy Ritchie crew for Fountain of Youth — an Apple TV+ production — working in the camera and electrical department across international sets. That experience confirmed what he already knew: the gap between regional production and global craft is smaller than the industry pretends, and it closes fastest when someone is willing to travel, learn, and deliver.

Today RASMY operates as a bridge. Cairo for instinct, cost and cultural depth. China for scale, hardware and manufacturing precision. Hong Kong as the corridor between them. The company produces commercial, automotive and destination films, and is expanding into private AI infrastructure and agent systems — because the same logic that makes a great film (clarity, rhythm, restraint) makes a great product.

He is 26. He does not plan to slow down.
