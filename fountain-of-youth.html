Fountain of Youth

Apple TV+Directed by Guy RitchieCamera & Electrical Department2024

Fountain of Youth is a feature film directed by Guy Ritchie for Apple TV+, starring John Krasinski and Natalie Portman. Pola Rasmy joined the production in the camera and electrical department, working across international locations as part of a global crew.

The shoot demanded the kind of precision only a large-scale production can teach: managing power distribution across massive sets, coordinating with multiple camera units, and maintaining visual continuity under relentless schedules. It was the first time Pola operated inside a Hollywood pipeline at this scale — and it changed how he thinks about every project since.

The lesson was simple and expensive: global craft is not a mystery, it is a standard. The gap between what regional productions accept and what international sets require is mostly process, communication, and refusing to cut corners on the fundamentals — light, sound, and story.
